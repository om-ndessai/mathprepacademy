# AMC 12 Question Style Guide

_Distilled from the real 2022-2024 AMC 12 A/B exams (AoPS wiki archive). Bank content must be original._

HOUSE STYLE — ORIGINAL AMC 12 PRACTICE SET (hard rules)

**Global invariants.** Every problem must be (1) fully solvable from text alone — no figures, ever; any geometric configuration must be verbally airtight ("In the xy-plane…", named vertices, stated parallelism/tangency); (2) strictly original — never a near-copy of a real AMC problem (change structure, not just numbers); (3) calculator-free, with every number engineered for clean exact work: factorable quadratics, recognizable Pythagorean triples, radicals that collapse, bounds where a relevant perfect power just barely misses; (4) unambiguous — close every loophole with terse parentheticals: "(not necessarily distinct)", "(non-degenerate)", "chosen uniformly and independently at random", "where m and n are relatively prime positive integers".

**Stem voice.** Givens first in declarative sentences; the ask is always the final sentence and always interrogative — "What is…?", "How many…?", "For how many…?", "Which of the following…?" Never imperative ("Find", "Compute" are banned). Formal register: introduce objects with "Let… be…" or "Suppose that…"; constraints ride on "such that/where/with" clauses. Stems run 1–4 austere sentences; length beyond that must be definition or worked example, never scenery. Nonstandard terms are defined inline, completely, before use (italicized if novel); intricate conditions get a parenthetical worked example — for the hardest conditions, one satisfying AND one failing instance with reasons. Standard canon (Vieta, law of cosines, roots of unity, log laws) is assumed silently; anything beyond it gets a "Recall that…" gloss. Weave this set's year-number into 4–6 problems as a load-bearing constant (a bound, an exponent, a numeral), never as decoration.

**Story vs. abstract balance.** Q1–10: at most 4–5 story shells, each one clause thick, strippable in a single read, zero decorative data; first names only (mnemonic/alliterative), no surnames, no personalities. Q11–20: at most 2 thin props; predominantly abstract. Q21–25: fully abstract unless the narrative IS the mathematical structure (e.g., a two-stage random procedure). A story may carry one deliberate decoy datum only when its irrelevance is the tested insight.

**Reasoning-step targets** (a step = one committed decision or technique application, not bookkeeping). Q1–10: 1–4 steps — exactly one gating insight (an identity, a symmetry, a reframing), then 30–60 seconds of clean arithmetic; grinding must be possible but slow. Ramp inside the band: Q1–7 single-tool, Q8–10 may demand full casework or a small linear system. Q11–20: 3–6 chained steps where the setup/substitution carries most of the difficulty; each problem hides exactly one nonobvious reframe, and two-domain fusion (logs×geometry, probability×Vieta, counting×valuations) is the band signature. Q21–25 are near-AIME: 5–9 steps requiring an INVENTED representation (closed form, per-prime system, bijection, governing Diophantine relation), plus a hidden second branch or validity filter (degenerate cases, out-of-range candidates, boundary events) that a first-pass solution silently misses, plus an exhaustiveness burden — brute force must be infeasible within 3 minutes.

**Answer-extraction wrappers.** Use on ~40% of problems, concentrated in Q11–25: "sum of the digits of…", "units digit of…", "remainder when divided by 10", "m + n where m/n is reduced", "greatest integer less than or equal to" (spelled out), "which interval contains…". Purpose: normalize magnitudes, sever backsolving, permit huge or irrational true answers. Any answer that is huge, transcendental, or trivially backsolvable MUST be wrapped.

**Choice conventions.** Exactly five options, one line: $\textbf{(A) } X\qquad\textbf{(B) } Y\qquad…$ — standardize on "\textbf{(A) }" spacing. Numeric choices in strictly ascending order; homogeneous in kind, form, and units within a problem (units live in the stem, never the choices). Fractions and radicals exact; decimals only for inherently decimal answer sets. At most one categorical-choice problem (options in natural order) and at most one verbal escape-hatch option (slot E) per set. No "None of the above". Correct letters need not be balanced across the set.

**Distractor engineering.** Every distractor must be the exact output of a specific, complete-feeling wrong solution path — write the generating bug down while authoring. Required patterns: (1) the boundary/off-by-one sibling of the truth, adjacent to it; (2) the value of the most natural correct partial step (the premature stopping point); (3) on count problems, correct-count-minus-one-forgettable-family per distractor, likeliest omission nearest the answer; (4) at least once per set, a compound of two bugs; (5) on floor/estimation problems, every value the answer could take under sloppy bounds — consecutive integers. Keep all five options tight in magnitude so estimation eliminates nothing; the correct answer must never be the roundest or most conspicuous option (a suspiciously clean truth camouflaged among elaborate options is encouraged once). On residue wrappers, offer the complete feasible set (e.g., {1,3,5,7,9}). Q23–25 shift philosophy: a flat opaque cluster (consecutive or consecutive-odd values) with no attributable errors, forcing the complete computation.

**Notation.** All numerals in stems in math mode ("$6$ numbers"). Segments $\overline{AB}$; triangles $\triangle ABC$; degrees $^\circ$ (bare trig arguments are radians); log bases subscripted, or declare "where $\log$ denotes the base-10 logarithm"; $n$ for positive-integer unknowns, $x$ real, $z$ complex, $k, j$ indices; indexed families $a_1, a_2, \ldots$ with orderings stated inline ($a \le b \le c$); large numbers comma-grouped $338{,}550$; currency as \$ escaped inside math per repo convention. Display math \[…\] reserved for the one centerpiece expression or system per problem; everything else inline. Set-roster notation $\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$; open intervals $(p, q)$.
