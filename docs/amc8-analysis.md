# AMC 8 Analysis — Requirements, Curriculum, and How This App Maps to It

_Researched and written July 13, 2026. Target: the January 2027 AMC 8._

## Executive summary

The AMC 8 is a 25-question, 40-minute, multiple-choice (A–E) middle-school math
competition run by the MAA each January. Scoring is 1 point per correct answer with **no
penalty for wrong answers or blanks** (max 25) — students should answer every question.
The 2027 competition window is **January 21–27, 2027**, and students must take it
**in person at a registered host site** (school, university, learning center, or math
circle) — individual/at-home registration does not exist.

For a rising 4th grader, the realistic and motivating first-year goal is the
**Achievement Roll: score 15+ in grade 6 or below** — national recognition that is only
available to young students. Honor Roll (top 5%) required 21/25 in 2026 and cutoffs are
rising every year.

## 1. Exam format and rules

| Attribute      | Value                                                                                                          |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Questions      | 25, multiple choice, five options (A–E)                                                                        |
| Time           | 40 minutes                                                                                                     |
| Scoring        | +1 correct, 0 wrong or blank; no guessing penalty; max 25                                                      |
| Calculators    | **Prohibited** (also phones, smartwatches, compasses, protractors, graph paper)                                |
| Allowed aids   | Writing utensils, blank scratch paper, rulers, erasers                                                         |
| Eligibility    | Grade 8 or below **and** under 15.5 years old on competition day (raised from 14.5 in the 2026 cycle)          |
| Attempts       | Once per year                                                                                                  |
| Administration | In-person only, digital (browser, MAA platform) or print-and-scan — host's choice; remote proctoring is banned |

Sources: MAA program page and 2026 Teacher's Manual
(maa.org/student-programs/amc/, maa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/2026-AMC-8-Teachers-Manual.pdf),
MAA competition policies (maa.org/student-programs/amc/maa-american-mathematics-competitions-policies/).

## 2. January 2027: dates, registration, cost

- **Competition window: January 21–27, 2027** (host picks one day within the week).
- Registration is by a school/organization "Competition Manager" — **parents cannot
  register students directly, and homeschools cannot host**. Homeschooled students are
  eligible but must test at a host site (their local school, a university, a math
  circle, or a commercial learning center that accepts outside students, typically for
  a ~$5–25 seat fee).
- Institutional fees (from maa.org/amcreg/): Early Bird **$55 by Oct 28, 2026**;
  Regular $75 by Jan 5, 2027; Late $115 by Jan 14, 2027 (returning hosts only), plus
  $25 per bundle of 10 student seats (~$2.50/student).

**Action item:** ask the students' school (or a local learning center) in
**early fall 2026** whether they will host the AMC 8, before the October 28
early-bird deadline. MAA's host lookup and the AoPS community are the standard ways to
find nearby sites.

| ------------------------ | -------------------- | ---- | ---- | ----- | ----- | ------ |

| Achievement Roll         | Grade ≤6 scoring 15+ | 15   | 15   | 15    | 15    | 15     |
| National average         | —                    | 9.74 | 8.3  | 10.54 | 11.75 | 12.42  |

Cutoffs were cross-verified across five independent trackers (Ivy League Education
Center, Areteem, SMO, ASDAN, Ace Academy); 2025/2026 averages are secondary-sourced
because MAA's statistics portal was unreachable during research. Participation runs
roughly 250,000–280,000 students per year worldwide.

**The trend matters:** percentile cutoffs have risen ~4 points since 2023 as prep
intensity grows. The app's mock-exam feedback bands use the 2025/2026 levels
(DHR ≈ 23+, HR ≈ 19+, Achievement 15+).

## 4. Curriculum and difficulty structure

**Official MAA topic list** (maa.org/student-programs/amc/): counting and probability,
estimation, proportional reasoning, elementary geometry including the Pythagorean
theorem, spatial visualization, and interpreting graphs and tables, with later
questions touching beginning algebra (linear/quadratic functions, coordinate geometry).

**Typical distribution** (unofficial, varies significantly by year — e.g., 2020 was
heavily arithmetic; 2025 had 5 number-theory questions):

| Area                                           | Typical share of 25 questions |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Arithmetic, ratios, word problems, pre-algebra | 8–10                          |
| Geometry & spatial visualization               | 5–7                           |
| Counting & probability (+ statistics)          | 4–7                           |
| Number theory                                  | 2–4                           |

**Difficulty ramp** (consistent across analyses): questions run easy → hard.
Roughly Q1–10 are accessible to a well-prepared elementary student, Q11–20 are the
middle tier, and **Q21–25 are the differentiators** — AoPS's competition ratings place
AMC 8 Q21–25 in the same difficulty tier as AMC 10 Q11–20. The hardest problems often
overlap MATHCOUNTS-style ideas.

**Practical score mapping** (inference from the ramp + cutoffs): mastering Q1–15
reliably ≈ Achievement Roll (15); adding most of Q16–20 ≈ Honor Roll (19–21); the top
1% requires cracking Q21–25.

## 5. An 18-month plan for a rising 4th grader (→ Jan 2027)

1. **Now – Dec 2026:** finish the elementary foundation (Beast Academy 4/5 is the
   standard on-ramp), take the app's **Quick Diagnostic**, then cycle topic quizzes —
   weakest topic first. Gentler contests (MOEMS Division E, Math Kangaroo) build test
   stamina.
2. **Fall 2026:** secure a test site before the Oct 28 early-bird deadline. Begin AoPS
   Prealgebra topics as ready; drill counting/probability and number theory — the areas
   schools cover least and the app tracks separately.
3. **Nov 2026 – Jan 2027:** weekly timed mocks in the app, then real past papers
   (legal sources: the AoPS contest wiki and live.poshenloh.com/past-contests, both
   with MAA permission). Strategy drills: never leave blanks, bank Q1–15 quickly,
   budget remaining time for Q16+.
4. **Goal ladder:** first mock ≥10 → winter ≥13 → test day **15+ (Achievement Roll)**.
   Anything more is upside; a 4th grader can chase Honor Roll in grades 5–8 with four
   more attempts.

**For a student tutor:** the topic quizzes double as tutoring diagnostics — assign a
quiz, read the per-topic breakdown and explanations together, then reassign after a
teaching cycle. A high schooler's own AMC 10 prep also benefits from the Q21–25 tier
(AMC 10 mid-range difficulty).

## 6. How the app maps to the exam

- **Taxonomy** (`packages/core/src/taxonomy.ts`): 8 topics mirroring the MAA list —
  arithmetic, number theory, algebra/patterns, geometry, counting, probability, data
  analysis, logic/spatial.
- **Difficulty tiers**: easy ≈ AMC 8 #1–10, medium ≈ #11–20, hard ≈ #21–25. The mock
  exam (`mock-01`) enforces the real ramp: 10 easy, 10 medium, 5 hard in order.
- **Scoring** (`packages/core/src/scoring.ts`): AMC 8 rules exactly — +1/0/0, no
  penalty; feedback bands calibrated to 2025/2026 cutoffs.
- **Timing**: 40-minute mock, 15-minute topic quizzes, with countdown and auto-submit.
- **Question bank**: 48 original AMC 8-_style_ questions written for this app. Real
  past AMC 8 problems are **MAA-copyrighted**, so the bank is original content;
  official past papers (AoPS wiki, Po-Shen Loh's LIVE archive) should be used directly
  for full-fidelity practice, and can be added later behind proper attribution.
- **Integrity**: answers and explanations never leave the server during a test;
  grading is server-side (`apps/api`).

## 7. Caveats and things to re-verify in fall 2026

- 2027 registration deadlines/fees were published at maa.org/amcreg as of July 2026 —
  re-check before registering.
- 2025/2026 cutoffs and averages come from reputable trackers, not MAA primary pages
  (which were intermittently down during research).
- Topic-distribution percentages are third-party classifications; year-to-year variance
  is large.
- MAA moved the AMC 8 from November to January in the 2021–22 cycle (there was no 2021
  AMC 8) — older prep materials referencing November dates are outdated, as are
  references to the 14.5-year age limit (now 15.5).
